    The core story

    The core launch notification. Every pack starts here, whatever the stakeholder.

    What's coming

    Product A is a once-monthly subcutaneous treatment for adults with moderate to severe Condition X. An application was submitted to the MHRA in March 2026, with a decision expected in Q1 2027.

    Why it matters

    Who it's for

    Adults whose disease is not adequately controlled after first-line therapy. We estimate around 8,400 eligible patients in England, or roughly 150 per million population.

    Population and pathway

    Where it fits

    Expected at the second-line stage of the current NICE NG000 pathway. Specialists in secondary care would start treatment, with ongoing supply through homecare.

    Formulary and prescribing

    Planned transition

    Product A is intended to replace Product B. Product B will remain available for at least 12 months after launch. No patient will need to switch before a clinical review.

    Supply and tariff

    What happens next

    Your account contact can share the modules relevant to you, brief your formulary or medicines optimisation committee, and update you as MHRA and NICE milestones are confirmed.
