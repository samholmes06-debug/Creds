    What NHS organisations need to know about Product A

    What's coming

    Product A is a once-monthly subcutaneous treatment for adults with moderate to severe Condition X. A marketing authorisation application was submitted to the MHRA in March 2026, with a decision expected in Q1 2027.

    Why Product A matters →

    Who it's for

    The anticipated licensed population is adults whose disease is not adequately controlled after first-line therapy. We estimate around 8,400 eligible patients in England, or roughly 150 per million population.

    Patient population and pathway →

    Where it fits

    Product A is expected to sit at the second-line stage of the current NICE NG000 pathway. Treatment would be initiated by specialists in secondary care, with ongoing supply through homecare.

    Formulary and prescribing →

    Planned transition

    Product A is intended to replace Product B, an existing treatment from the same company. Product B will remain available for at least 12 months after launch so that clinicians can review and move patients in a planned way. No patient will need to switch before a clinical review.
